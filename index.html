Get in tune in three steps.

No complicated setup. Choose your instrument and let the live needle guide you to the right pitch.

⌕

Choose what you play

Select from 30 instrument presets. For string instruments, choose a specific open string or switch to chromatic mode.

♩

Tap Start tuning and allow microphone access in your browser. Use a quiet room for the steadiest reading.

↔

Follow the needle

The note and cents indicator show whether your sound is flat, sharp, or centered. Adjust until it reads in tune.
